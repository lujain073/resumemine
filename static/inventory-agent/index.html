Your pharmacy’s stock, checked like a pharmacist would.

Upload the inventory and sales files. The agent finds medicines about to run out, batches about to expire unsold, and money tied up on the shelf — then writes tonight’s report.

  • Days of cover, stockout dates and lead-time alerts for every medicine
  • Expiry risk per batch, with First-Expired-First-Out sell-through
  • Reorder quantities with the calculation shown — never placed automatically
  • Losses at purchase cost only, and missing data flagged instead of guessed

Run the agent

CSV or Excel. Column names are matched automatically — see the template below.

File template

Inventory: one row per batch. Only drug name and current stock are required; every extra column improves the analysis.

drug_nameMedicine name and strength (required)
current_stockUnits on hand for this batch (required)
daily_salesAverage units sold per day — not needed if you upload sales history
expiry_dateYYYY-MM-DD, DD/MM/YYYY or MM/YYYY
batch_numberBatch or lot number
purchase_priceCost per unit — used for every financial figure
selling_priceRetail price per unit (informational)
supplier, lead_time_daysWho supplies it and how many days delivery takes
min_stock, max_stockYour minimum and maximum stock levels

Sales history: date, drug_name, quantity_sold — one row per medicine per day.


    

    

      
How the agent decides

      
    
        
  1. Days of cover = sellable stock ÷ average daily sales (last 28 days of history). Expired units are excluded.
    2. 
        
  2. Critical: cover shorter than supplier lead time · High: out within 3 days · Warning: within 7 days · Reorder notice: at or near minimum stock.
    3. 
        
  3. Reorder quantity = demand × (lead time + review period) + safety stock − stock, capped at maximum stock. Safety stock needs 14+ days of history.
    4. 
        
  4. Expiry: Critical ≤ 30 days, High ≤ 60, Moderate ≤ 90, per batch. Unsold quantity assumes earliest-expiry batches are dispensed first.
    5. 
        
  5. Money uses purchase price only. Without it, the agent says losses cannot be calculated.
